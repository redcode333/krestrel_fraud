BUSINESS FACTS (from ops-policy.pdf and email thread):
- The investigation desk can review at most 40 claims per month. Kestrel gets about 750 claims a month.
- A fraudulent claim paid costs the full claim amount. A genuine claim held for review costs Rs 380 goodwill. A service contact costs Rs 260.
- Until 30 Apr 2026 every claim needed partner inspection. From 1 May 2026, claims under Rs 2,000 are auto-approved without inspection.
- Legacy Zoho system ran until 30 Sep 2025; CRM after. Zoho could not store blanks, so undecided cases were exported as 0. CRM rows have real blanks (undecided).
- Policy section 9: only Zoho resolution events were stored in UTC. Those events are not in our CSVs, so this probably does not affect us. Verify, mention in one line, move on.
- Policy section 10: data must not be uploaded to public repos. Never commit anything from data/. Commit only code, README, memo, predictions.csv, and model artefacts that contain summaries only (no customer rows).
- The client asked for ">97% accuracy". Accuracy is a misleading KPI because fraud is about 1% of claims. Our real metric is rupees of fraud stopped per claim reviewed with a 40-per-month budget.
