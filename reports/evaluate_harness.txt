Evaluation harness self-test with dummy scores (src/evaluate.py). No model is involved.
Review budget 40 claims a month; a genuine claim held costs Rs 380; every reviewed claim costs Rs 260 (assumption).

Folds (time axis = first_submitted_at, decided claims only):
  Fold A: train < 2026-05-01, validate May 2026 - STRESS TEST of the regime change (the new pattern starts on 1 May, so training holds no new-regime history)
      train 9724 claims (105 fraud); validate 709 claims, 14 fraud (1.97%) from 6 partners, out of 312 partners with claims
  Fold B: train < 2026-06-01, validate June 2026 - PRIMARY fold for model selection
      train 10433 claims (119 fraud); validate 713 claims, 22 fraud (3.09%) from 8 partners, out of 316 partners with claims
  Pooled: fold A's May scores + fold B's June scores
      no fit of its own; validate 1422 claims, 36 fraud (2.53%) from 10 partners, out of 360 partners with claims
  Old design: train < 2026-05-01, validate May-June 2026 (what a model that has not seen the new regime scores)
      train 9724 claims (105 fraud); validate 1422 claims, 36 fraud (2.53%) from 10 partners, out of 360 partners with claims

CAUTION: the validation windows hold very few frauds (36 in total, from 10 partners). One partner more or less in a resample moves every top-40 number a lot, so the 90% intervals below are wide. Read them as rough ranges, not precise estimates.

What to expect from the dummies:
  fold_A: picking 40 claims blind should find about 40 x 1.97% = 0.79 frauds
  fold_B: picking 40 claims blind should find about 40 x 3.09% = 1.23 frauds
