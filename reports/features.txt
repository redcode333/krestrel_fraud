====================================================================================================
Feature list
====================================================================================================

Label-based, frozen at the cut-off (rules A, B):
   1. partner_new_fraud_count: Confirmed frauds this partner had in the new regime (from 1 May 2026) that were decided before the cut-off.
   2. partner_new_claim_count: Decided claims this partner had in the new regime before the cut-off.
   3. partner_new_rate_smoothed: Partner's new-regime fraud rate pulled towards the overall new-regime rate: (frauds + 5 x overall rate) / (claims + 5). A partner with no decided claims gets exactly the overall rate.
   4. partner_rate_ratio: partner_new_rate_smoothed divided by the overall new-regime rate: above 1 = worse than average, below 1 = better. Exactly 1.0 for a partner with no decided new-regime claim, at every cut-off. Models use this instead of the raw smoothed rate.
   5. partner_fraud_count_old: Confirmed frauds this partner had before 1 May 2026 (decided before the cut-off). Kept separate so the model can learn it is weak.
   6. partner_has_new_history: 1 if the partner has at least one decided new-regime claim, 0 if the model knows nothing about it in the new regime.

Label-free, rolling over earlier claims (rule C):
   7. partner_claims_last_7d: Claims this partner submitted in the 7 days before this claim.
   8. partner_claims_last_30d: Claims this partner submitted in the 30 days before this claim.
   9. partner_small_share_last_30d: Share of those 30-day claims that were under Rs 2,000 (0 if the partner had none; see partner_claims_last_30d).
  10. partner_avg_amount_last_30d: Average amount of those 30-day claims in rupees (0 if the partner had none).

Known at submission:
  11. partner_age_days: Days between the partner's onboarding and this claim.
  12. is_new_partner: 1 if the partner was onboarded on or after 1 Oct 2025.
  13. customer_prior_claims: The customer's earlier warranty claims.
  14. photo_attached: 1 if a photo was attached.
  15. claim_amount_inr: Amount claimed, in rupees.
  16. amount_to_list_price: Amount claimed divided by the product's list price.
  17. is_small: 1 if the claim is under Rs 2,000 (auto-approved without inspection since 1 May 2026).
  18. warranty_used_share: days_since_purchase divided by the warranty length in days (warranty_months x 30).

One-hot groups (categories fixed from partners.csv and products.csv, so train, test and the app agree):
  partner_type_* (3 columns): One column per partner type (service centre, franchise, freelance technician).
  city_* (18 columns): One column per partner city.
  family_* (7 columns): One column per product family.

Total: 46 columns.
Considered and left out: hour, weekday, day of month and claim_description phrases (no signal, see the checks above); n_submissions, serial-repeat features, inspector_note and partner_inspected (rule D).
